SELF-CHECK
==========
Q1: Does the analyser handle the examples it was designed for?
    Yes — the four prefix-substitution rules succeed on all regular
    m + vowel, ki-, ch- and identity (Class 9) forms.
    Result: 12/15 PASS.

Q2: Are its limitations clearly explained?
    Yes — the error log records three genuine failures caused by
      (a) consonant-initial stems after 'm-' (mke, mche, mguu);
      (b) the prefix-only model's inability to represent
          stem-internal change (mguu -> miguu).
    The reliance on lexical tags and the absence of nasal
    assimilation are also documented.
